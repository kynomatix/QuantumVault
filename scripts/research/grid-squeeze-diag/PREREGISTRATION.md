## ATR definition

Use the shipped Wilder ATR(14), initialized on the same 100 closed bars as the finder. Rank the current raw price-unit ATR against 182 preceding daily observations at the same UTC hour (one per day in the trailing 26 weeks), each computed on its own 100 closed bars. Exclude today's ATR from the reference; empirical midrank = (strictly lower + half equal)/182. Compressed: percentile <0.20; normal: 0.20 through 0.80 inclusive; elevated: >0.80. Require all 182 values on the same chosen venue with valid contiguous finalized candles. Otherwise record UNAVAILABLE, never silently shrink 26 weeks. Also publish a predeclared ATR/last-close normalized sensitivity using the same histories and boundaries. Do not pool the two definitions.

## Frozen-band age definition

The shipped 100-hour structural range cannot measure 7-14-day or older age. A launch-defined band tested backwards is tautological. Therefore this independent diagnostic uses the entry-rule lesson (form then freeze a band before testing it): freeze the high/low envelope of the 14 days from launch minus 42 days through launch minus 28 days, then observe the following 28 days without moving either boundary. Age is the uninterrupted terminal run of hourly CLOSES inside that fixed band, counted only after the freeze. A break resets age; later re-entry starts a new episode. If the latest close is outside, age=0 and the label is under 7 days, with OUTSIDE separately disclosed and an inside-only sensitivity. Age <7 days; 7 through 14 days inclusive; >14 days. No break for 28 days is right-censored at >=28 days. Missing/invalid 42-day history is UNAVAILABLE. This measures persistence in a pre-existing fixed reference band, not the age of the finder's structural band or of an objectively identified market regime.
