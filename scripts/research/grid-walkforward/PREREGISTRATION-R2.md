## Calendar and procedure

Training starts March 1, 2022. A fresh seeded bot starts each Monday at 00:00 UTC, runs seven days, then closes all remaining inventory with taker fees and 0.05% adverse slippage. The last complete week ends October 19, 2026. Select on March 1, June 1, September 1 and December 1, starting March 1, 2023; the last September quarter is partial. A week uses the selection known at launch, even if its end crosses the next selection date. At selection, include only weeks launched within the previous twelve calendar months whose full outcome ended on or before the selection date. There is no outcome overlap across train and forward windows.

Choose one GLOBAL option separately at each builder fee. Each market has an equal fixed capital sleeve; weekly portfolio return is the arithmetic mean of its sleeve returns, then portfolios compound across weeks. Score each option by median weekly portfolio net return in training; tie-break by lower protective-stop frequency, then lexicographically ascending canonical option ID. Retain every ranking, training boundary and choice. No thresholds, candidates or selectors change after seeing returns.

## Fixed menu and execution model

The full Cartesian product has 384 options per fee: range week/month; 6/10/14/20 planner intervals (the inherited grid-count convention); stop-distance multiplier 1/1.5 beyond the appropriate range boundary using widerGeometry; step_back/repeat_all; long/short/sign of the trailing 30-day close return (zero is long); and exactly one filter none/R1/ATR/finder. Investment is 1,000 quote units per sleeve, leverage 1x. Builder fee is 0.10% or 0%, added to maker 0.015% and taker 0.04% on every fill including terminal exits. These are fixed grid-bot fee scenarios on OKX proxy observations, not claims about actual historical OKX account tiers.

R1 is the inherited touches-to-middle rule: at least two touches near each edge of the preceding 28-day range, touches spaced at least one day apart, and position from 0.4 through 0.6. ATR uses the earlier R3 rule: hourly ADX below 20 and the latest normalized 14-hour mean true range above the 20th percentile of 28 daily samples. Finder uses the shipped hourly 100-bar qualifier. Signal computation uses 840 contiguous finalized past hourly candles; no forming or future candle. Thirty-day sign uses the preceding closed price and the close exactly 720 hours earlier.
