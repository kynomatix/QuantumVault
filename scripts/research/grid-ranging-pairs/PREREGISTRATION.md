## Scores and selection
Aggregate complete UTC daily bars. Compute at most three scores on preceding six and twelve calendar months, excluding selection day:
1. ER = abs(last close-first close)/sum(abs(consecutive close differences)); zero denominator gives zero. Lower is ranging. Primary ranking uses only twelve-month ER.
2. Containment = share of closes within window median close +/- twice window mean daily true range; true range=max(high-low,abs(high-previousClose),abs(low-previousClose)), requiring preceding close. Higher is ranging. Descriptive only.
3. Mean-reversion count = sign crossings of close minus trailing 30-close simple mean, including that completed day's close; ignore zeros until next nonzero sign. Divide crossings by days/30.4375. Higher is ranging. Descriptive only.
Select March/June/September/December 1 from 2023-03-01 through 2026-09-01. Require preceding twelve months. Sort ascending ER then ASCII symbol; floor(N/3) lowest=ranging, floor(N/3) highest=trending, all N=all. Freeze next three months. No replacement after execution rejection. Compute separately in A and A+B. No score or cutoff selection from outcomes.
